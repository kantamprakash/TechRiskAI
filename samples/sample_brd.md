# Customer Loyalty Platform – Business Requirements Document

## 1. Background
RetailCo wants to replace its paper-based loyalty cards with a mobile loyalty platform for 2 million members.

## 2. Business Requirements
BR-01: Customers earn points on every purchase in store and online.
BR-02: Points must be visible in the mobile app in real-time after a purchase.
BR-03: Existing member balances will be migrated from the current AS400 loyalty system.
BR-04: Customers can redeem points at checkout via the third-party payment gateway.
BR-05: The platform stores customer PII including name, email, date of birth and address.
BR-06: Personalised offers will be generated using machine learning.

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements
NFR-01: The system must support 50,000 concurrent users during Black Friday.
NFR-02: Availability of 99.99% is required.
NFR-03: Security requirements are TBD.

## 4. Timeline
Go-live is planned for 1 November, before the holiday season.
